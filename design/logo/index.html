Reeforge logo

An atoll seen from above: a ring of reef around a lagoon, left open where the ember sits. The ring is the loop that keeps improving (reforge); the ember is the forge, the spark that closes it. One symbol for the whole hub; each app adds its name after the wordmark.

Colour on light backgrounds; reversed (sky ring, light wordmark) on ocean and dark; black or white only when colour is not possible (stamps, embossing, single-colour print, photos).

Symbol and app icon

Clear space and minimum size

Keep a clear space around the logo equal to the ring thickness × 2 (about a third of the symbol height). Minimum sizes: symbol 16 px, logo 96 px wide, Reeforge Finance logo 120 px wide.

Don't

Also: no shadows, glows or outlines on the logo; no busy photos behind it without a calm area; never retype the wordmark, use the files.
